Venue and Location

IFIP SEC 2027 will be hosted in Linköping, Sweden, from 14–16 June 2027.

Conference Venue

Linköping University

The conference will take place at Linköping University, Campus Valla, in Building B.

Finding the Conference Venue

The conference will take place in Building B at Campus Valla. Please consult the official campus map to locate the building and the appropriate entrances. Most conference activities will take place in Ada Lovelace which is most conveniently accessible from entrance 27.
